# Connected carousels
Build a single N×W by H scene and clip it into N slides. Shared background coordinates, product scale and lighting must stay identical across slices. Do not rebuild each slide independently with slightly different crops.

Useful connections, chosen to fit the approved style:
- A hero object extends 10–25% across the boundary; the next view remains recognizable in its own slide.
- A substantial background plane or photographic surface flows across the seam, if the style already supports it.
- A repeated structural feature continues physically, with product details leading the eye across the boundary.
Hero placement is a design choice, not a rule: do NOT default to centering the product on the seam. Offer at least one option with the hero off-center, large and anchored low in slide 1 (e.g. bottom-left), crossing the seam so its far end is cropped into slide 2 and bleeds toward the slide-2 edge. Slide 1 keeps copy in its upper area; slide 2 keeps headline and spec card in the clear zone on the other side of the hero. Check that the hero's seam-crossing part never covers text and that each footer/label stays clear of the product silhouette. A dark product on a dark scene needs a glow, rim light or lighter plane behind it.
Keep titles and important specifications inside their own slide. Do not split model names, logos or essential labels. Each slide must remain useful alone.

See `layout-patterns.md` for the named patterns. Two photos: slide 1 primary product with key facts; slide 2 alternate angle plus additional specs. Avoid making slide 2 an empty crop. Keep both photos distinguishable. Overlap only where it preserves product readability; do not merge two motors/devices into an impossible object. A decorative thin line alone is usually too weak a connection. Adding a new geometric panel changes the style and is inappropriate when the user requested only a connection fix.

Before rendering, mark the seam in review guides and check the actual clipping. Preview connected and separated. After feedback, inspect the selected version, retain approved style and copy, and change only the requested layout issue. Never silently export a rejected or older version.
