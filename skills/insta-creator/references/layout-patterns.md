# Layout patterns
Offer different patterns across options; pick by how many usable photos the user supplied. All patterns are one continuous 2160×1440 scene clipped into slides (see `carousels.md`). Never default to a centered product.

## 1. Seam hero (1 photo)
One large product crossing the seam, centered or off-center. Off-center variant: anchor the product low in slide 1 (e.g. bottom-left), let its far end continue into slide 2. Slide 1 copy sits in the upper clear area; slide 2 headline and spec card sit on the opposite side of the product. Dark product on a dark scene needs a glow, rim light or lighter plane.

## 2. Photo per slide (2–3 photos)
Use a different supplied photo per slide: slide 1 the clean hero shot, slide 2 a close-up or alternate angle, slide 3 (if supplied) a detail or in-use shot. Rules:
- Each photo is a real, separate view; never duplicate or fake an angle. If only one angle exists, use pattern 1.
- The next slide's photo may peek 10–15% across the seam into the previous slide (its leading edge visible at the right edge of slide 1) to invite the swipe. Never cover text or the other product, and never merge two products into one object.
- Slide 1: hero photo, name, one-line hook, a compact spec strip. Later slides: larger/closer photo, headline, one spec card with substantive details. Each slide must read alone.
- Photo size may differ per slide (small clean hero, then a big close-up) to create rhythm.
- 3 photos = 3 slides in one 3240×1440 scene. Keep all slides the same size.

## 3. Big cropped hero + small alternate angle (2 photos)
Slide 1: a very large hero photo anchored low-right, tilted, with its far end crossing the seam and cut by it, so the product feels bigger than the frame. Copy stays in the upper-left; a faint oversized ghost label (e.g. a key number such as "4CH", low contrast, behind the product) can fill the empty lower-left. Slide 2: the cropped continuation of the hero at the left edge, a stack of 2–4 spec cards in the upper right, and a second, smaller photo of a different angle in the lower area with a short caption and the CTA. The seam cut must land on a non-essential part of the product (no logos, labels, ports you are talking about); the same product must be fully readable in the smaller slide-2 photo.

## Recurring structure that works
Brand row top (logo + hairline rule continuing across the seam) → small uppercase eyebrow (brand / category) → headline in two tones (white + one accent) → one sentence → product → glass spec card → footer row (domain · swipe cue · CTA). Footer text never touches the product silhouette.
