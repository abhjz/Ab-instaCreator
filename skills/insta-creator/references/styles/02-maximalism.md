# Maximalism

## Composition
Dense but deliberate layers around a clear focal point.

## Typography
One primary headline; supporting fragments in distinct scales.

## Materials
Patterns, stickers and collage texture in controlled groups.

## Quality check
Do not obscure product or essential copy; crowding is not random clutter.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
