# Retro

## Composition
Period-inspired composition with one recognizable era.

## Typography
Era-appropriate display treatment within brand font constraints.

## Materials
Subtle grain, aged print or analog color treatment.

## Quality check
Do not degrade product accuracy or combine unrelated eras arbitrarily.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
