# Aurora

## Composition
Luminous soft fields guide attention through a dark or quiet scene.

## Typography
Bold readable headline with restrained labels.

## Materials
Broad multicolor or brand-tinted light gradients.

## Quality check
Glows must not wash out copy or alter true product colors.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
