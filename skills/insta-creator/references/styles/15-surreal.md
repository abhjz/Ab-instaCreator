# Surreal

## Composition
One conceptual visual contradiction with a readable subject.

## Typography
Simple headline supports the visual idea.

## Materials
Impossible scale or environment with coherent lighting.

## Quality check
Do not imply invented product capability through the metaphor.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
