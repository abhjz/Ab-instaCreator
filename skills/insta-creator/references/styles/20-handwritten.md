# Handwritten

## Composition
Human annotations around a clean primary composition.

## Typography
Handwritten accents paired with clear main information.

## Materials
Marker strokes, paper notes and informal arrows.

## Quality check
Keep Arabic shaping correct; do not fake handwriting with distorted glyphs.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
