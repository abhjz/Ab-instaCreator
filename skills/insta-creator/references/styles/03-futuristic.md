# Futuristic

## Composition
Asymmetric technical hero, modular information blocks.

## Typography
Crisp sans type, lining numerals, compact data labels.

## Materials
Controlled glow, metallic depth and fine technical marks.

## Quality check
Avoid random HUD noise and decorative lines behind every product.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
