# Swiss

## Composition
Rigorous grid and asymmetrical balance.

## Typography
Shared left edges, strong size hierarchy, restrained line lengths.

## Materials
Flat planes, clean rules and purposeful negative space.

## Quality check
Never stretch letters to justify a title or confuse alignment with identical width.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
