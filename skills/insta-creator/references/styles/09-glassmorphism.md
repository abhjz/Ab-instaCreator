# Glassmorphism

## Composition
Layer readable translucent panels over a lit product scene.

## Typography
Clear sans with contrast and generous panel padding.

## Materials
Frosted blur, low-opacity fills, fine highlight borders.

## Quality check
Avoid opaque grey rectangles and low-contrast text on glass.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
