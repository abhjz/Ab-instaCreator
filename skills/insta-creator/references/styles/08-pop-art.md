# Pop Art

## Composition
Comic impact with a burst or grid framing the product.

## Typography
Bold outlined headline, short sticker labels.

## Materials
Halftone dots, hard shadows, thick outlines.

## Quality check
Keep stickers and patterns away from product details and reserved poll area.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
