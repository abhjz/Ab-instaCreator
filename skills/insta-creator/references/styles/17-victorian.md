# Victorian-inspired

## Composition
Ornamental frame around a simple focal subject.

## Typography
Decorative headings only if the brand allows; clear body.

## Materials
Engraving-inspired rules and symmetrical details.

## Quality check
Avoid ornamental overload and obscure small type.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
