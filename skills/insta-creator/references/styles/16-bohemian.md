# Bohemian

## Composition
Organic balance and gentle earthy structure.

## Typography
Relaxed hierarchy without sacrificing readability.

## Materials
Natural shapes, warm textures and handmade motifs.

## Quality check
Do not let the reference palette override the user brand.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
