# Minimalism

## Composition
One dominant subject, generous negative space, restrained palette.

## Typography
One short headline; clear hierarchy and shared edges.

## Materials
Flat fields, subtle shadow, few fine rules.

## Quality check
Do not leave an empty slide with no purpose or make tiny text.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
