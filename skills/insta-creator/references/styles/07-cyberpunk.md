# Cyberpunk

## Composition
Angular high-contrast geometry and one assertive product hero.

## Typography
Bold compact sans, sparse technical labels.

## Materials
Sharp edges, restrained electronic accents and noise.

## Quality check
Do not assume cyberpunk always needs a neon city or illegible glitch text.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
