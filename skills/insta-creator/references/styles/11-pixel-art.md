# Pixel Art

## Composition
Consistent pixel grid and strong simple silhouettes.

## Typography
Readable pixel-inspired display, normal body if needed.

## Materials
Stepped edges and intentional limited-color shading.

## Quality check
Avoid inconsistent pixel sizes and unreadable labels.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
