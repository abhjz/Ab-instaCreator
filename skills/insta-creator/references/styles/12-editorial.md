# Editorial

## Composition
Strong column grid, deliberate margins and image-led storytelling.

## Typography
Headline, deck and caption hierarchy with controlled contrast.

## Materials
Paper-like space, fine rules, sparse annotations.

## Quality check
Do not force tiny magazine text into phone graphics.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
