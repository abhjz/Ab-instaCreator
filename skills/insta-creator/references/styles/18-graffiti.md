# Graffiti

## Composition
Energetic marks framing a recognizable product.

## Typography
Expressive headline with clean supporting text.

## Materials
Spray, stencil and rough edges.

## Quality check
Never modify the brand logo into graffiti without permission.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
