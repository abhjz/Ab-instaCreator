# Collage

## Composition
Layered cutouts with purposeful overlaps and a coherent focal image.

## Typography
Editorial hierarchy with limited contrasting scales.

## Materials
Paper edges, shadows, torn strips.

## Quality check
Keep brand assets intact; never use unlicensed photo fragments.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
