# Clay

## Composition
Rounded sculptural forms and friendly spatial composition.

## Typography
Soft but legible type hierarchy.

## Materials
Matte rounded shapes, gentle directional shadows.

## Quality check
Do not replace precise product geometry with an inaccurate toy.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
