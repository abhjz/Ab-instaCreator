# Vector Art

## Composition
Clear geometric forms and deliberate silhouette hierarchy.

## Typography
Clean type and simplified supporting labels.

## Materials
Editable paths, flat colors and controlled gradients.

## Quality check
Do not redraw a real product inaccurately or call raster images editable vectors.

For a carousel preserve continuity and slide-safe text. For a story rebuild hierarchy vertically and preserve interface/sticker space. Adapt these rules to the supplied brand; do not merely swap background colors between options.
